Interactive Guide & Controls

Welcome to The Genesis of Life, a 3D embryological simulation tracing human conception from the initial ovulation event up to Week 7 of embryonic development.

3D Navigation

  • Rotate View: Left-click and drag anywhere on the 3D viewport.
  • Zoom: Scroll with your mouse wheel or pinch on a trackpad/touchscreen.
  • Pan: Right-click and drag (or two-finger drag).
  • Anatomical Hotspots: Click any floating cyan pin or structure tag to inspect it.

Branching Pathways

  • Pregnancy Journey: Follow the path of conception: Insemination → Sperm Odyssey → Fertilization → Cleavage → Blastocyst → Implantation → Weeks 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.
  • Menstruation Branch: Toggle the top branch button to witness what happens if the egg does not encounter sperm within 24 hours (oocyte degeneration, corpus albicans, endometrial shedding).

Procedural Audio Engine

Click the speaker icon at top right to activate the microscopic soundscape and listen to the real-time cardiac heartbeat during Weeks 4 through 7.
